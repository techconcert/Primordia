## Why Primordia reads as it does

1. **Creature is small against flora.** Trees are many times taller than the player. Canopies sit high, so the player walks under a canopy shell. Trunks are thin, so the scale impression comes from height, not girth.
2. **Small things are very small.** Critters and fruit read as trivially small. This makes the player feel little and makes epics feel huge.
3. **Epics dominate.** At 20+× player height, an epic overlaps several trees. Its big silhouette is the top-of-food-chain cue.
4. **Camera.** Close third-person, behind and slightly above the creature. It frames the creature at mid-screen, so the environment is judged relative to the creature. [E] Exact distance unknown.
5. **Tribal scale-up.** The Tribal Stage view pulls back and animals look bigger than before [S]. Huts are about 2× villager height so the village reads as a settlement, not a dollhouse [E].

## Addendum: observations from the user's screenshots (docs/ref_primordia_creature_1.png, _2.png)
Google image grids of the original Creature stage. Sourced by eye, higher confidence than the [E] estimates above.
- Wild animals are SMALL in frame next to flora. In nearly every in-world shot (nest shots, "Epic Creature Requirements", meadow shots) trees are ~3-5x the height of a typical adult animal; some canopies leave the top of the frame. Trunks are thin; the canopy is a big flat/umbrella mass high above.
- Ground cover is tiny: grass tufts, flowers and bushes reach only about ankle-to-knee of an adult creature, in dense scatter, so the creature reads as larger than the plants but tiny next to trees.
- Camera sits well back and above, so creatures fill only ~10-20% of frame height; this scale-down is a big part of the "good proportions" feel.
- Nests are wide low bowl/rock mounds roughly 2-3x the width of one creature, with eggs about half a creature's height.
- Epics are dark, huge creatures; they dwarf normal animals (and are about tree height or taller), but only a few exist.
- Herds in the first screenshot (hills) are many small creatures far apart; variation between species is moderate (about 0.5x to 2x of the player), not 5x.
- Tribal huts (nest/village shots) are about 1.5-2x villager height, wide and squat.
Implication: normalise all non-epic animals to ~0.5-2x the player (player ~1.6-2.2 u), trees 6-9 u (3-5x), ground plants <0.6 u, epics ≤ ~tree height.
